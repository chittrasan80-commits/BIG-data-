E-Commerce Customer Segmentation using Big Data

CCS334 Big Data Analytics mini project · Team 5 · UCI Online Retail · SDG 8

Customers are scored on Recency, Frequency and Monetary value (RFM) and grouped into segments such as Champions, At Risk and Hibernating, so a retailer can target each group differently.

Pipeline

Hadoop MapReduceStreaming job computes RFM per customer
Apache HiveHiveQL analytics and NTILE-based RFM scoring
PySparkKMeans clustering, k chosen by silhouette score
CassandraSegments served by customer and by segment
